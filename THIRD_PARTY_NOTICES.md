## Stockfish

Stockfish is a powerful, free chess engine developed by a community of volunteers. It is not affiliated with any chess brand and is distributed free of charge under the GPL.

- **Repository:** https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish
- **Stockfish.js wrapper:** https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js
- **License:** GNU General Public License v3

ChessMate runs Stockfish entirely in your browser (in a Web Worker) — no server is involved, and none of your games are sent anywhere.
